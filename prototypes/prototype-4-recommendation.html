Recommendation rationale

  • From P1: Label is "I'm done" — matches the user's internal state. Not "Stop."
  • From P2: Fixed footer — always visible, mobile-safe. No scroll needed.
  • From P3: Button earns visual weight over time. Early in session: dim, honest. After N cards: accent purple elevation with one-time hint text.
  • Not from P4: Inactivity overlay omitted — too clever, risks feeling patronizing.

Key tradeoff: The button is technically present from card 1, but visually dormant — it won't distract early learners, and it will be seen by returning users who know to look for it. The elevation moment is the signal that the app now considers you entitled to stop.
