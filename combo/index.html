Color Combinations

In Magic: The Gathering, every color pair and triple has a name. These 20 combinations — drawn from Ravnica's guilds and Alara/Tarkir's three-color factions — each represent a distinct philosophy and play style.

Allied Guilds

Learn these names

Two-color pairs where the colors are neighbors on the color wheel. Allied colors share fundamental values, making their combination feel natural and harmonious.

  • Azorius
  • Dimir
  • Rakdos
  • Gruul
  • Selesnya

Enemy Guilds

Learn these names

Two-color pairs where the colors are opposites on the color wheel. Enemy colors have conflicting philosophies, creating dynamic tension in their combination.

  • Orzhov
  • Izzet
  • Golgari
  • Boros
  • Simic

Wedges

Learn these names

Three-color combinations named after the clans of Tarkir. Each wedge centers on one color and adds its two enemy colors, creating an unusual alliance.

  • Abzan
  • Jeskai
  • Sultai
  • Mardu
  • Temur

Shards

Learn these names

Three-color combinations named after the shards of Alara. Each shard centers on one color and adds its two allied neighbors, forming a natural extension.

  • Bant
  • Esper
  • Grixis
  • Jund
  • Naya
