# Small Arc Studio

### Company Charter

---

## Who We Are

Small Arc Studio is a collaborative software consultancy practicing **Graceful Development**.

We build meaningful systems in small, deliberate arcs — each one complete, observable, and aligned with a larger evolving direction.

We do not attempt to predict the entire circle.  
We move it forward.

---

## Our Core Belief

Complex systems cannot be fully known in advance.

Plans evolve.  
Understanding deepens.  
Context shifts.

But progress can still be reliable.

A **small arc** is bounded, intentional, testable, and real.  
It fits into a broader direction that may change — without becoming waste.

We deliver arcs that stand on their own.

---

## Our Philosophy: Graceful Development

Graceful Development means:

- Doing the right work, not the most work.
- Delivering the smallest meaningful increment.
- Learning before scaling.
- Understanding the domains deeply.
- Designing for change rather than defending against it.
- Making reasoning visible.
- Preserving adaptability.

We avoid thrash.  
We avoid speculative complexity.  
We avoid output theater.

We build with composure.

---

## How We Work

### 1. Direction Over Prediction

We establish direction instead of rigid long-term certainty.  
We revise direction when learning demands it.

### 2. Arcs as Units of Delivery

An arc:

- Has a clear intention.
- Produces observable change.
- Is testable and verifiable.
- Reduces risk or increases clarity.
- Leaves the system stronger than before.

The larger plan may evolve.  
The arc must deliver.

### 3. Observability Is Required

If users cannot see the difference, operators must.

Every arc produces evidence:

- Working behavior
- Passing tests
- Distributed traces revealing new flow
- a dashboard measuring outcomes
- Measurable improvements

Invisible work is incomplete work.

### 4. Quality Is Built In

We do not ship what we cannot verify.

Completion requires:

- Passing tests
- Clear acceptance criteria
- Verification by our team in a browser
- Recorded reasoning
- A visible next step

---

## Our Relationship With Clients

Clients are collaborators in the arc.

We:

- Ask focused, thoughtful questions.
- Offer options with tradeoffs.
- Make clear recommendations.
- Adapt when new understanding emerges.
- Speak candidly about risk.

We do not inflate certainty.  
We do not hide complexity. The value of software is the complexity it encapsulates.

---

## Our Roles

We operate as a cohesive team:

- **Project Lead** — aligns direction and synthesis.
- **Domain Expert(s)** — research each domain and understand it.
- **Designer** — expresses the purpose of the app in its UI.
- **Architect** — shapes boundaries and long-term coherence.
- **Developer** — builds small, clean, intentional increments.
- **Observability Engineer** - makes each feature visible and measurable.
- **Tester** — ensures behavior is provable and reliable.
- **Librarian** — records decisions, reasoning, and the evolving narrative.

Quality is shared responsibility.

---

## Our Standard

An arc is complete when:

- It works.
- It is verified.
- It is observable.
- Its reasoning is documented.
- It fits the current direction.
- We are proud to put our name on it.

Small arcs move living systems forward.

That is our work.
