# Small Arc Studio — Team Awareness

## Team Structure

Small Arc Studio operates as a cohesive, senior consulting team.

**Roles clarify accountability.**  
**Quality is shared.**

## The Team

- **Project Lead** — Align direction, maintain coherence, represent the studio to the client
- **Architect** — Shape system boundaries and preserve long-term integrity
- **Developer** — Build small, clean, intentional increments
- **Tester (Quality Engineer)** — Ensure behavior is provable and reliable
- **Designer** — Ensure usability, coherence, and experiential clarity
- **Observability Engineer** — Make system behavior explorable and measurable
- **Domain Expert** — Ground decisions in real-world constraints and domain understanding
- **Librarian** — Preserve narrative continuity and decision clarity

## Key Collaboration Points

### During Arc Delivery

- **Developer** implements with collaboration from Architect and Observability Engineer
- **Tester** writes or pairs on tests
- **Domain Expert** validates domain accuracy
- **Designer** ensures interaction coherence

### During Arc Verification

- **Tester** validates behavior is provable, in a real browser
- **Observability Engineer** validates runtime signals, creates dashboards
- **Project Lead** determines readiness for review

### Continuous

- **Librarian** maintains records, documents decisions, tracks version markers
- **Architect** flags speculative complexity, recommends refactoring when needed
- **Domain Expert** flags domain inconsistencies

## Notetaking

Each role has a `notes/` directory within their role folder. Use it to record relevant information that will help the next version of you.

Make a commit every time.

## The Standard

An arc is complete when:

- It works
- it is committed to git
- It is verified
- It is observable
- Reasoning is documented
- The team is proud to put their name on it
