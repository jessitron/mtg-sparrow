OG Image Design Options — MTG Colors

Three design directions for the og:image (1200×630px). Each shown at full size and at thumbnail scale (600×315). These images appear when someone shares mtgcolors.quest on Discord, Slack, Twitter/X, iMessage, etc.

Option 1 — Logo-Centric
Large spiral dominates. Clean, minimal. The spiral is the identity — everything else serves it. Strong at any scale. The five-color accent bar grounds the mana-color theme without complexity.

Designer notes:
  • Option 1 is the safest choice — timeless, clean, legible at all sizes. Best for initial deployment.
  • Option 2 is most immediately "MTG" to players — they recognize the pip symbols instantly. Most colorful.
  • Option 3 has the richest texture and most world-building. The guild collage signals depth of content. May need stronger vignette if guild PNGs have light backgrounds.
  • All options use the colored spiral from favicon.svg. The og:image will need to be generated as a static PNG — these HTML mockups are for design review only.
  • Font note: GoudyMediaeval is not loaded here (would need the TTF files). Georgia is used as a close substitute for design review. The final PNG generation would need the actual font.
