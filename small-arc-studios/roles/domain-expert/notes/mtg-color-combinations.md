## The Five Colors of Magic

Magic: The Gathering has five colors of mana, each with a single-letter abbreviation. They are always listed in the canonical **WUBRG** order (pronounced "woo-berg"):

| Color | Letter | Mana Symbol | Philosophy |
|-------|--------|-------------|------------|
| White | W | {W} (sun) | Order, law, community, protection |
| Blue  | U | {U} (water drop) | Knowledge, logic, manipulation, perfection |
| Black | B | {B} (skull) | Power, ambition, self-interest, death |
| Red   | R | {R} (fireball) | Freedom, emotion, impulse, destruction |
| Green | G | {G} (tree) | Nature, growth, instinct, tradition |

**Why "U" for Blue?** Because "B" was already taken by Black. "U" is the next unique letter in "blUe."

---

## The Color Pie (Color Wheel)

The five colors are arranged in a circle (clockwise): **W → U → B → R → G → (back to W)**.

This arrangement defines two critical relationships:

- **Allied colors** = adjacent on the wheel (share philosophical overlap)
- **Enemy colors** = across from each other (philosophically opposed)

### Allied Pairs
W-U, U-B, B-R, R-G, G-W

### Enemy Pairs
W-B, U-R, B-G, R-W, G-U

This allied/enemy distinction matters because it determines how the three-color combinations are categorized (shards vs. wedges).

The color pie was designed by Richard Garfield to ensure diversity in gameplay and prevent any single deck from having access to every tool.

## Colorless (0 colors)

Colorless is not a color — it is the absence of color. Artifacts, Eldrazi creatures, and lands are commonly colorless. In Commander, a colorless deck can only use cards with no colored mana symbols.

Colorless mana has its own symbol: {C} (a diamond shape), introduced in Oath of the Gatewatch. The basic land "Wastes" produces colorless mana.

**For the Sparrow Deck:** Colorless is worth mentioning but probably not a primary learning target since it doesn't have a "name" to learn.

## Two-Color Guilds (10 combinations)

All 10 two-color pairs are named after the **Guilds of Ravnica**, a plane (world) in MTG where each guild represents a two-color institution. These names were established in the original Ravnica block (2005-2006) and are universally used by the community.

### Allied-Color Guilds

| Colors | Guild Name | Ravnica Role |
|--------|-----------|--------------|
| WU | **Azorius** | Senate (law & order) |
| UB | **Dimir** | Spy network (secrecy & manipulation) |
| BR | **Rakdos** | Cult of entertainment (chaos & spectacle) |
| RG | **Gruul** | Clans (primal rage & nature) |
| GW | **Selesnya** | Conclave (community & growth) |

### Enemy-Color Guilds

| Colors | Guild Name | Ravnica Role |
|--------|-----------|--------------|
| WB | **Orzhov** | Syndicate (religion & commerce) |
| UR | **Izzet** | League (science & experimentation) |
| BG | **Golgari** | Swarm (death & rebirth) |
| RW | **Boros** | Legion (military & justice) |
| GU | **Simic** | Combine (biology & evolution) |

**Community usage:** Guild names are the standard way to refer to two-color decks in all contexts — casual, competitive, Commander, Limited, and Constructed. A player saying "I'm playing Izzet" means their deck is blue-red. These are the most commonly used and well-known color combination names.

## Three-Color Combinations (10 total)

Three-color combinations are split into two categories based on their relationship on the color wheel:

### Shards (5) — from Shards of Alara block (2008-2009)

A shard is a color plus its **two allied colors** (three consecutive colors on the wheel). Named after the five fragmented worlds of the plane Alara.

| Colors | Shard Name | Central Color | Description |
|--------|-----------|---------------|-------------|
| GWU | **Bant** | White | Chivalry, honor, exalted |
| WUB | **Esper** | Blue | Artifice, control, sphinx |
| UBR | **Grixis** | Black | Undeath, cruelty, demons |
| BRG | **Jund** | Red | Savagery, dragons, devour |
| RGW | **Naya** | Green | Behemoths, nature, might |

**Alternative names:** Shards don't really have alternative names. The Alara names are universally accepted.

### Wedges (5) — from Khans of Tarkir block (2014-2015)

A wedge is a color plus its **two enemy colors**. The shape formed on the color wheel is a wedge/acute triangle. Named after the five clans of the plane Tarkir.

| Colors | Wedge Name | Central Color | Pre-Tarkir Names | Letter Abbrev. |
|--------|-----------|---------------|-------------------|----------------|
| WBG | **Abzan** | White | Junk, Necra, Dark Naya | — |
| URW | **Jeskai** | Blue | American, Raka, UWR | USA, UWR |
| BGU | **Sultai** | Black | BUG, Ana | BUG |
| RWB | **Mardu** | Red | Dega, Borzhov | — |
| GUR | **Temur** | Green | RUG, Ceta | RUG |

**Important note on wedge names:** Before Khans of Tarkir (2014), wedges had no official names. The community used various informal names:
- **Letter abbreviations**: BUG (Black-Blue-Green), RUG (Red-Blue-Green), USA/UWR (Blue-White-Red)
- **Older plane names**: Necra, Raka, Ana, Dega, Ceta (from Apocalypse, 2001)
- **Slang**: "Junk" for WBG, "American" for URW

After Khans of Tarkir, the clan names (Abzan, Jeskai, Sultai, Mardu, Temur) became the standard, though you'll still hear "BUG" and "RUG" from veteran players, and "Junk" occasionally for Abzan.

## Four-Color Combinations (5 total)

Four-color combinations are the **least standardized** naming area. There is no single universally accepted naming convention.

### Nephilim Names (from Guildpact, 2006)

| Colors | Missing Color | Nephilim Name |
|--------|--------------|---------------|
| WUBR | No Green | **Yore-Tiller** |
| UBRG | No White | **Glint-Eye** |
| BRGW | No Blue | **Dune-Brood** |
| RGWU | No Black | **Ink-Treader** |
| GWUB | No Red | **Witch-Maw** |

### "Sans" / "Non-" Convention (most common in practice)

Since the Nephilim names never caught on widely, most players identify four-color combos by the **missing color**:

| Colors | Missing Color | Common Names |
|--------|--------------|-------------|
| WUBR | No Green | Sans-Green, Non-Green, Not-Green |
| UBRG | No White | Sans-White, Non-White, Not-White |
| BRGW | No Blue | Sans-Blue, Non-Blue, Not-Blue |
| RGWU | No Black | Sans-Black, Non-Black, Not-Black |
| GWUB | No Red | Sans-Red, Non-Red, Not-Red |

### Commander 2016 Names (used by some players)

Commander 2016 introduced four-color commanders, providing another set of potential names, but these also haven't become widely adopted as color combination names.

**Community usage:** Four-color names are rarely used in practice because four-color decks are uncommon outside of Commander. When they are referenced, "sans-[color]" or "non-[color]" is the most practical and commonly understood approach. The Nephilim names are known by enfranchised players but not widely used.

**For the Sparrow Deck:** Four-color combinations could be an advanced/optional learning tier. The naming is genuinely unsettled, so the app might teach the Nephilim names while noting the "sans-X" convention.

## Learning Priority for the Sparrow Deck

Based on community usage frequency and name stability:

1. **Tier 1 — Must Know (10):** The 10 Guild names (two-color). Used constantly in all formats.
2. **Tier 2 — Should Know (10):** The 5 Shard + 5 Wedge names (three-color). Used frequently, especially in Commander and Standard.
3. **Tier 3 — Nice to Know (5+1):** Four-color Nephilim names and WUBRG. Rarely needed but fun to know.
4. **Bonus:** Legacy wedge names (BUG, RUG, Junk, American) — useful for understanding older content and veteran players.
