## Overview

Strixhaven: School of Mages is a plane in MTG set on the world of Arcavios. It is home to the most prestigious magical university in the Multiverse, Strixhaven, which comprises five colleges. The original Strixhaven set released April 2021; **Secrets of Strixhaven** returns to the plane with a tabletop release of **April 24, 2026** (prerelease April 17).

Key design fact: Unlike the Ravnica guilds (which are built around the **similarities** of their two colors), the Strixhaven colleges are built around the **tension and conflict** between their two colors. Each college has a distinct academic focus that emerges from that philosophical opposition.

The set logo and plane symbol is an owl — "Strix" is a genus of owls.

## The Five Colleges

### 1. Silverquill (White/Black) — WB

**Ravnica equivalent:** Orzhov Syndicate

**Academic focus:** The magic of language — words both written and spoken. Philosophy, rhetoric, debate, poetry. Their magic manifests as ink and light.

**Mascot:** Inklings — tiny sentient globs of animated ink

**Elder Dragon Founder:** Shadrix Silverquill (wields light and shadow through communication)

**Flavor:** The art students of language. Silverquill mages are fierce debaters, philosophers, and writers. Think "law school meets creative writing MFA." They are competitive, eloquent, and often sharp-tongued.

**SOS Keyword — Repartee:** Triggers whenever you cast an instant or sorcery that targets a creature, regardless of who controls it. Repartee abilities resolve before the spell itself (making stack interaction awareness important). Represents the college's culture of verbal sparring — always getting the last word.

**SOS Commander Deck:** "Silverquill Influence" — helmed by Killian, Decisive Mentor (a 2/4 Human Warlock who taps and goads creatures when enchantments enter under your control). Strategy: political manipulation and Goading.

**Key distinction from Orzhov:** Orzhov is about the extraction of wealth and power through religious/bureaucratic hierarchy — ghosts, debt, and spirits. Silverquill is about the power of ideas and rhetoric — ink, light, and words. Both use W/B but Orzhov manipulates social/financial systems while Silverquill manipulates perception and argument. Orzhov wants your money; Silverquill wants to win the debate.

---

### 2. Prismari (Blue/Red) — UR

**Ravnica equivalent:** Izzet League

**Academic focus:** The fusion of elemental magic and performance art. Prismari mages see no difference between magic and art. Giant, showy elemental displays are their medium of expression.

**Mascot:** Art Elementals — constructs of elemental energy shaped by creative intent

**Elder Dragon Founder:** Galazeth Prismari (masters elemental energy through art)

**Flavor:** The performing arts / fine arts department. Bold, dramatic, expressive. Where Izzet is about scientific experimentation and mad-scientist engineering, Prismari is about artistic vision and expression. Think: "What would happen if MFA students had fire magic?" Bright, over-the-top costumes. Instants and sorceries as canvases.

**SOS Keyword — Opus:** Triggers when you cast an instant or sorcery. The effect is bigger or better if you spent 5 or more mana to cast that spell. Represents the grand, ambitious scale of Prismari artistic performance — go big or go home.

**SOS Commander Deck:** "Prismari Artistry" — for players who want to cast expensive instants and sorceries with maximum impact.

**Key distinction from Izzet:** Izzet is steampunk inventors; Prismari are performance artists. Both cast lots of instants and sorceries, but Izzet's motivation is scientific curiosity and chaotic invention, while Prismari's is aesthetic expression and spectacle. They are "cut from the same Spellslinger cloth" but with very different aesthetics and cultural identity.

---

### 3. Witherbloom (Black/Green) — BG

**Ravnica equivalent:** Golgari Swarm

**Academic focus:** Biomancy — the biology of life and death. Necromancy, botanical magic, and the study of how living things decay and regenerate.

**Mascot:** Pests — small adorable creatures with morbid associations

**Elder Dragon Founder:** Beledros Witherbloom (represents life and death)

**Flavor:** The biology and biochemistry department, with a dark academic twist. Witherbloom students are mottled biologists who study the life cycle including death. Imagine biology lab meets gothic botanical garden. The vibe is a bit morbid but scholarly rather than purely sinister.

**SOS Keyword — Infusion:** Triggers whenever you gain any amount of life during the turn. Represents Witherbloom's obsession with the interchange between life force and death — draining to gain, sacrificing to heal.

**SOS Commander Deck:** "Witherbloom Pestilence" — helmed by Dina, Essence Brewer. Strategy: sacrifice and lifegain, winning through draining opponents.

**Notable SOS card:** Sprout Swarm with Witherbloom's Affinity synergies can create infinite creature tokens as early as turn three.

**Key distinction from Golgari:** The most mechanically similar pair — both are about life/death cycling and graveyard usage. The key distinction: Golgari is about using the graveyard in many different ways (recursion, undergrowth, scavenging). Witherbloom specifically focuses on the **life/death interchange** — gaining life from death, sacrificing creatures to gain life. Golgari is underground sewer culture; Witherbloom is gothic academic research lab. More scholarly, less apocalyptic.

---

### 4. Lorehold (Red/White) — RW

**Ravnica equivalent:** Boros Legion

**Academic focus:** Historical magic — archaemancy. Lorehold mages dig up the past, animate historical spirits and artifacts, and study history as a living discipline. Magic from and about history.

**Mascot:** Spirit Statues — the college animates historical figures as spirits inhabiting stone statues

**Elder Dragon Founder:** Velomachus Lorehold (channels order and chaos)

**Flavor:** The history/archaeology department. Passionate scholars obsessed with the past. Where Boros is all military action and forward momentum, Lorehold is about uncovering what came before and putting it back to work. Think: Indiana Jones as a mage — adventure, discovery, ancient power.

**SOS Keyword — Flashback:** The graveyard-cast mechanic. A set defined by digging up the past and putting it back to work gets the mechanic that lets you cast spells from the graveyard. Deeply on-flavor. In SOS, Lorehold grants Flashback (and potentially Miracle) to instants and sorceries.

**SOS Commander Deck:** "Lorehold Spirit" — graveyard synergies layered onto Spirit tribal and token generation. Goes in a direction W/R rarely explores.

**Notable SOS card:** Lorehold, the Historian — described as a "spell-slinging dream with huge combo potential."

**Key distinction from Boros:** This is the sharpest distinction of all five colleges. Boros is all about attacking — military aggression, justice through force, battalion and mentor keywords. Lorehold is the **opposite** of that aggressive forward momentum: it looks backward, recurs the past, and animates history. Same colors (R/W), completely different identity. Boros charges forward; Lorehold digs backward. If Boros is the military, Lorehold is the archive and museum.

---

### 5. Quandrix (Green/Blue) — GU

**Ravnica equivalent:** Simic Combine

**Academic focus:** Mathematical and theoretical magic — studying the fundamental patterns and fractals underlying nature. Mathemagics. They wield power over the mathematics of reality itself.

**Mascot:** Fractals — mathematical constructs made manifest as creatures

**Elder Dragon Founder:** Tanazir Quandrix (masters reality and abstraction)

**Flavor:** The mathematics and theoretical physics department. Ingenious, abstract, and working in theoretical spaces. Where Simic was about hands-on biological experimentation and adaptation, Quandrix is about pure theory and the mathematics underneath everything.

**SOS Keyword — Increment:** Whenever you cast a spell, if the amount of mana spent to cast it is greater than the power or toughness of the creature with Increment, that creature gets a +1/+1 counter. Represents the mathematical scaling principle — Quandrix creatures grow when you invest more mana, like a function with increasing returns.

**SOS Commander Deck:** "Quandrix Unlimited" — X-spells, +1/+1 counters, Fractals, and Hydras. Reprints include Hardened Scales and Ozolith, the Shattered Spire.

**Notable SOS card:** Mathemagics — described as "very on-brand Quandrix content."

**Key distinction from Simic:** Both are about evolution and growth through G/U. But Simic is about biological experimentation — adapting living organisms, hands-on mutation of flesh. Quandrix is about **theoretical mathematics** — working in abstract spaces, studying patterns and fractals, mathematical principles applied to nature. Simic is the biology lab; Quandrix is the mathematics department. Simic is Darwin; Quandrix is Euler.

## New Mechanics in Secrets of Strixhaven

### Set-Wide Mechanic: Prepare

A new mechanic replacing the original set's Learn/Lesson system. Prepare cards allow you to cast spells attached to them while fulfilling a "Prepared" condition. When a creature is "prepared," a copy of its attached spell appears in exile and can be cast while the creature remains in play. Example: Emeritus of Ideation can cast Ancestral Recall (Power Nine) because his ability lets him enter prepared.

### Mystical Archive Returns

Like the original Strixhaven, the new set includes a Mystical Archive — a special series of powerful historical instant and sorcery reprints in special frame treatment.

## Mnemonic Hooks for the Learning Tool

The most valuable learning asset here is the sharp distinction between each college and its corresponding guild. These differences are what make the colleges memorable as a second layer of knowledge on top of guilds.

**Silverquill vs. Orzhov (WB):**
- Orzhov = religious extortion, ghosts, debt, money, power
- Silverquill = words, rhetoric, debate, ink, light
- Hook: "Same colors, different weapons: Orzhov uses your soul as collateral; Silverquill uses your reputation."

**Prismari vs. Izzet (UR):**
- Izzet = science experiments, mad inventors, steampunk chaos
- Prismari = performance art, elemental spectacle, expression
- Hook: "Same spellslinging energy, different motivation: Izzet asks 'Does it work?'; Prismari asks 'Does it wow?'"

**Witherbloom vs. Golgari (BG):**
- Golgari = graveyard recursion, undergrowth, sewer ecology
- Witherbloom = lifegain from death, sacrifice for healing, gothic biology
- Hook: "Both are about death feeding life, but Golgari is the ecosystem; Witherbloom is the lab running experiments on it."

**Lorehold vs. Boros (RW):**
- Boros = military assault, forward aggression, justice by force
- Lorehold = archaeology, history, animating the past, graveyard recovery
- Hook: "Completely opposite directions: Boros charges forward; Lorehold digs backward. Same colors, opposite momentum."

**Quandrix vs. Simic (GU):**
- Simic = biological evolution, mutation, adapting organisms, counters
- Quandrix = pure mathematics, theoretical patterns, fractals, abstract scaling
- Hook: "Both grow with counters, but Simic mutates flesh; Quandrix solves equations. Simic is Darwin; Quandrix is Euler."

## Domain Accuracy Notes

- The set is confirmed to use the same five colleges from the 2021 original — no new colleges introduced
- Elder Dragons return as significant legendary cards (new versions)
- Mascots now appear as full creature cards, not just tokens
- The original Learn/Lesson mechanic is replaced by Prepare
- All five colleges now have their own distinct keyword (in 2021, all colleges shared the Learn mechanic plus individual themes)
- The set is instants/sorceries matters, like the original
